# **Victims of wage theft in New York City are disenchanted with a sluggish Labor Department**

## In this project, I ran multiple regressions (multivariate and single) to try and figure out *which* state wage theft cases gained priority in federal courts. This was particularly interesting to analyze since the U.S. DoL outlined the factors they use in deciding whether to take on a case — turns out they might not be abiding by their own standards.
